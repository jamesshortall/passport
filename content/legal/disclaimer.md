## App availability changes fast

The information on AppPassport — whether a given app works in a country, which
local alternative to use, how payments work, and whether a VPN is needed —
**can and does go out of date quickly.** Governments change rules, apps enter
and leave markets, payment rails shift, and VPN access is blocked or restored
with little warning.

## We are not your only source

AppPassport is a **convenience and starting point, not an authoritative or
official source.** Before you travel, and especially before you rely on any
single app abroad, please also check:

- Official government and embassy travel advisories for your destination.
- The app makers' own current availability information.
- Recent, local, on-the-ground sources.

## "Last verified" dates

Each app entry shows a **"last verified" date.** Treat older dates with extra
caution — a lot can change between then and your trip.
