This policy explains how AppPassport uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies include
`localStorage`, which we use to remember your cookie choice and your session.

## Categories we use

- **Strictly necessary.** Required for the site and sign-in to work (e.g. your
  authentication session). These are always on.
- **Preferences.** Remember choices such as your cookie-consent decision.
- **Analytics (optional).** Help us understand usage so we can improve the app.
  These are only set if you accept them in the banner.

## Managing your choice

When you first visit, a banner lets you **Accept**, **Reject**, or **Manage**
optional cookies. You can change your mind any time by clearing this site's
storage in your browser, which will bring the banner back.

## Third parties

Our authentication and database provider (Supabase) may set cookies strictly
necessary for sign-in. Any optional analytics provider we add will be listed
here.
