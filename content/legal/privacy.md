AppPassport ("we", "us") is part of The Travel Technician. This policy explains
what we collect and why.

## What we collect

- **Account information.** If you create an account, we store your email
  address and authentication details through our provider, Supabase.
- **Favorites.** If you save countries, we store which countries you saved,
  linked to your account.
- **Usage & cookies.** We use essential cookies to keep the site working and,
  with your consent, optional analytics cookies. See our
  [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
- **Feedback reports.** If you submit a "report outdated" note, we store the
  note and the entry it refers to. Anonymous reports are not linked to an
  account.

## How we use it

- To operate the site and your account.
- To review and improve the accuracy of our country data.
- To send you account and (if you opt in) update-digest emails.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service
providers that run the app (e.g. Supabase for the database/auth, and our email
provider for transactional email).

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by
contacting us at the address on [The Travel Technician](https://www.traveltechnician.info).

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via
[www.traveltechnician.info](https://www.traveltechnician.info).
